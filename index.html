About me

Hi, I'm Sabin Shrestha, a PCB designer and electrical engineering student from Dharan, Nepal. I enjoy turning an idea into a real, working circuit board.

I completed my +2 diploma in Electrical Engineering at Shree Public High School, Dharan, and continued my studies in the Electrical Engineering faculty at Purwanchal Campus. Alongside my studies, I work on PCB design, electrical installation, robotics and IoT projects.

I'm always learning and open to new projects, collaborations and learning opportunities.
